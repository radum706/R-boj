Behavior

Panels

New tabs open in the panel you're viewing; tabs opened from a page stay in their parent's panel. Deleting a panel moves its tabs to the first panel.

Appearance

Custom CSS for the sidebar. Classes: .row, .row.active, .title, .panel, #pinned. Variables: --row, --indent, --bg, --fg, --accent.

Snapshots

Restoring opens each saved window as a new window, keeping panels and the tree.

Shortcuts

Defaults: Alt+Shift+S open sidebar, Alt+Shift+←/→ switch panel, Alt+Shift+T new child tab. In the sidebar: ↑↓ switch tab, ←→ collapse/expand, Del close, / search, Ctrl/Shift-click to select several. Change them at brave://extensions/shortcuts.
